COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers to the questions we hear most. Still unsure? Call (562) 598-7813 and ask us directly.

Do you really offer free drain inspections?

Yes. Our free drain inspection uses camera technology to assess your drains and identify clogs, cracks, or damage, so you get an honest recommendation before spending anything.

Are you available for plumbing emergencies at night?

Yes, we are on call 24/7. Burst pipes, overflowing toilets, clogged drains, and water heater failures get a fast response day or night.

What is hydro-jetting and when do I need it?

Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour grease, roots, and scale from inside your pipes. It is the best fix for recurring clogs and slow drains that snaking alone cannot clear.

Can you fix a sewer line without digging up my yard?

Often, yes. Our sewer re-lining service is trenchless: a new liner is installed inside the old pipe, renewing it without tearing up your yard, driveway, or landscaping.

Do you repair and install water heaters?

Yes. We repair failing water heaters and install new tank and tankless units, sized to match your home and hot water demand.

Do you work on commercial properties too?

Yes. We provide commercial plumbing services including drain cleaning, pipe repairs, water heater installations, and full system maintenance for businesses of all sizes, with minimal downtime.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Los Alamitos and serve every city in Orange County, including Seal Beach, Cypress, Garden Grove, Huntington Beach, Anaheim, Irvine, and Costa Mesa.
